# KYC/AML Compliance Framework

## 1. Know Your Customer (KYC) Requirements

### 1.1 Individual Users
- Full legal name and date of birth
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driver's license, national ID)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, government document)
- Occupation and employment information
- Source of funds verification

### 1.2 Business Users
- Business registration documents
- Articles of incorporation/partnership
- Tax identification number (EIN/TIN)
- Beneficial ownership disclosure
- Business address and contact information
- Director/officer identification

### 1.3 Verification Process
- Document collection and verification
- Biometric verification (facial recognition)
- Address verification
- Background checks (where applicable)
- Ongoing monitoring and re-verification

## 2. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance

### 2.1 Transaction Monitoring
- Real-time transaction screening
- Suspicious activity detection
- Threshold-based alerts ($10,000+)
- Pattern analysis and anomaly detection

### 2.2 Sanctions Screening
- OFAC SDN list screening
- EU sanctions list screening
- UN sanctions list screening
- Ongoing monitoring for list updates

### 2.3 Reporting Requirements
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)
- Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)
- Regulatory reporting (FinCEN)
- Jurisdiction-specific reporting

## 3. Risk Assessment

### 3.1 Risk Levels
- **Low Risk:** Standard verification, routine monitoring
- **Medium Risk:** Enhanced verification, increased monitoring
- **High Risk:** Enhanced due diligence, transaction limits, senior review

### 3.2 Risk Factors
- Geographic location (high-risk jurisdictions)
- Transaction patterns (unusual activity)
- Business type (high-risk industries)
- Customer profile (PEP status, sanctions history)

## 4. Compliance Controls

### 4.1 Technical Controls
- Automated transaction screening
- Real-time monitoring systems
- Alert and escalation procedures
- Audit logging and retention

### 4.2 Operational Controls
- Staff training and certification
- Compliance policies and procedures
- Regular audits and assessments
- Incident response procedures

### 4.3 Documentation
- KYC files and records
- Transaction records
- Compliance reports
- Audit trails

## 5. Restricted Activities

Users are prohibited from:
- Money laundering and terrorist financing
- Sanctions evasion
- Fraud and financial crimes
- Illegal activities
- Market manipulation

## 6. Regulatory Compliance

- FinCEN regulations (31 CFR Chapter X)
- OFAC compliance (31 CFR Chapter V)
- State money transmitter laws
- International AML standards (FATF)
